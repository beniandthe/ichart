# Natural complete-chord data — bounded source follow-up

This is a read-only primary-source discovery result, not data acquisition,
license clearance, recognition accuracy, or proof that no other source exists.
No access gate was accepted, contact information shared, author contacted,
dataset downloaded, app/profile changed, or private holdout inspected.

## Requirements still not met by an acquired source

Natural complete chord writing; original online strokes; exact written spelling
and component/ownership labels; reliable writer/session IDs; separate fit,
development and genuinely held-out writers; clear terms suitable for the intended
use. Raster data or semantic chord strings may support different research, but
must not be described as satisfying this full contract.

## Primary findings

| Source | Established evidence | Remaining boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [JAZZMUS creator paper](https://arxiv.org/html/2509.05329v1) and [dataset card](https://huggingface.co/datasets/PRAIG/JAZZMUS) | Real handwritten complete chord imagery in scanned/photographed lead sheets; semantic MusicXML/**kern encodings. | No original online strokes. Published split is by piece, not demonstrated writer-disjoint. Semantic-equivalent spellings were not relabeled to every exact glyph variant. Card declares CC-BY-NC-4.0 and gated access/contact sharing. No training-use or commercial clearance is asserted here. Already identified in the earlier auxiliary-source audit. |
| [Janmohamed, UCF 2023 thesis record](https://stars.library.ucf.edu/honorstheses/1462/) | The primary abstract explicitly reports handwritten chord symbols collected from UCF students, separately from printed Real Book symbols. This is a newly located documented collection. | Public landing page exposes thesis PDFs, not an established dataset package. Complete-string versus component annotations, exact spelling, counts, writer IDs, strokes and data license could not be verified. PDF access returned 403 in the research tool. A documented collection is not an acquired usable corpus. |
| [HOMUS creator page](https://grfia.dlsi.ua.es/homus/) | Original musician-written 2D pen strokes with musician directories. | Isolated notation symbols, not natural complete chord names or the A–G chord-root letter alphabet. It cannot replace the complete-chord corpus. |
| [ChoCo official repository](https://github.com/smashub/choco) | Semantic/text harmony annotations in JAMS with annotation provenance. | No observed handwriting. Harmony annotators are not identifiable handwriting writers. |

The bounded search found no ready public source satisfying all of the above.
Do not silently substitute isolated glyphs, mathematical expressions, raster
images or semantic chord annotations for natural writer-disjoint Pencil input.
The UCF record is a concrete lead for a later user-authorized data-access inquiry,
not permission to contact its author or accept dataset terms now.

## Relation to the running experiment

The current synthetic bridge uses 896 original fit glyphs and 224 observed
development glyphs, reused across thousands of assembled chord strings. The
additional predeclared layout-cue probes remove two artificial input cues but
still do not add natural writing or new writers. None of these results can
complete the fresh natural-input, full-symbol, optional-learning-benefit,
application-runtime or device-acceptance requirements of the active goal.
