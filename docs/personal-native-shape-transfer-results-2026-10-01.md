# Native-shape auxiliary transfer: reject, stop this recipe

The single predeclared experiment actually completed. It did not meet the
personalization/writer trust guards. Do not promote, retune this recipe, change
its aliases/sampler/head/schedule, or use reserved writers to rescue it.

Scope: public isolated-character, already-observed development research. These
are not fresh writers, natural chords, confidence calibration, Swift/Core ML
parity or physical Pencil evidence. No app, profile, chart ink, signing,
install, commit, push or deployment changed in this pass. The recognition goal
remains open; this result is not a shipping milestone.

## What was different and what executed

The September 28 mapped-HASY continuation and frozen-five-output expansion
already ran and failed. The full-source audit did not establish new model
quality. This probe instead fitted all 369 native mathematical identities as an
auxiliary task, discarded that head, and fitted the unchanged 97-class UJI task.
It tested a source-plus-task package, not an isolated source/writer-count effect
and not missing chord-symbol recognition. The inference vocabulary stayed 97.

The [frozen protocol](personal-native-shape-transfer-protocol-2026-10-01.md)
was recorded before preparation/fitting. Both arms started with identical
seed-29 raster trunks/projections/BatchNorm. Independent seed-29 auxiliary
heads had 97 or 369 outputs. Both actual fits completed 5 x 248 pretrain updates
and 30 x 32 UJI updates: **2,200 per arm, 4,400 total**. The complete original
97-way head was restored exactly between phases, preserving learned features.
Batch-position plans and augmentation RNG histories matched; final UJI
augmented inputs were identical. Only final checkpoints were evaluated.

All 6,208 training embeddings per arm were reconstructed exactly from the
saved final checkpoints. Auxiliary initial states, pretraining checkpoints,
final schemas and same original UJI target raster/plans were reconciled. This
is production-helper-backed training-only reconstruction, not independent model
accuracy validation. No development/reserved features were used during fitting.

**28 synthetic tests passed**, warnings as errors, zero failures/skips. They
verify source, gradient, initialization/reset, artifact, reporting and evaluation
contracts. They do not measure recognition quality or app behavior.

## Source preparation and independent check

The audited 168,233 source rows remained in an explicit ledger. Fixed native
conflict/copy filtering left 166,311 rows; normalization conflict/copy filtering
left 166,216. Excluded rows: 1,181 native conflicts, 741 native copies, 74 adapted
conflicts and 21 adapted copies, **2,017 total**. All 369 classes remained.

The balanced selection had 31,744 exposures: **30,451 distinct source images**
and **1,293 repeated exposures**. Native labels were not aliased into chord
symbols; source user IDs remain unreliable, and no trajectories were invented.
The prior binary-inversion/crop/bilinear/center transform was reused unchanged.

The separate verifier independently reconstructed all ledger joins/exclusions,
representatives, salted selection order/cycles, class counts and all binary
targets/exposure fingerprints. It independently normalized all 30,451 selected
RGB images byte-for-byte. Unselected adaptations were ledger-reconciled, not
all re-decoded. Selected-HASY versus UJI query raster copies: **zero**.

HASY-derived weights remain local research only. ODbL provenance is retained;
commercial derived-weight distribution rights are unresolved. No shipping
eligibility is asserted by the source or model receipts.

## Frozen development result

Both checkpoints were fixed before inference. All **3,104 scheduled attempts**
were serialized before query truth or the original copy-exclusion ledger was
opened. All attempts were readable/finite; none was dropped. The query set is
776 session-two characters from eight previously observed writers; four exact
copies remain excluded in the common no-copy view, denominator **772**. All
97 competing labels, classes, writers, and taught/untaught strata are retained.

Those aggregates do not satisfy the trust screen:

- Personal versus the candidate's own generic rankings: core10 4 corrections,
  1 regression; catalog21 7 corrections, 3 regressions. Every regression was
  on an untaught character (zero untaught gains).
- Personal candidate/control writer deltas in the pinned order W04, W06, W08,
  W11, UPV-W35, W43, W47, W56: core10 `-2,-1,+2,+3,-1,-1,+1,+3`;
  catalog21 `-2,-1,+1,+4,-1,-1,+3,+2`. Four writers regress in both tasks.
- Both raw/no-copy views fail the same writer and untaught-harm guards. Their
  eight-condition arrays are `true,true,true,false,true,false,true,true`.
  The pre-existing non-ASCII35 stress guards pass; they cannot rescue failure.

Disposition: `reject-fixed-candidate-no-retuning`. This is modest aggregate
transfer with unsafe sparse personalization, not reliable across-writer
improvement. No superiority over the operational app baseline was established.
The twenty reserved UJI writers remain sealed. Private handwriting/answer
acceptance did not select this model, recipe or recognition rule.

The independent 112-line stdlib scorer reconstructed predictions plus the
pinned truth/copy joins before reading the production score. Every score field
matched: 3,104 attempts, 1,552 paired task/query joins, 776 raw/772 eligible,
all 97-class/writer/stratum summaries, paired IDs and all eight guards. No model
call or refitting was used for this reconciliation.
