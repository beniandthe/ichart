## Next implementation boundary

The personalized means being better than unrelated means is evidence of some
writer association; their losses to zero refute its sufficiency as this fixed
global correction. Improve the symbol/style representation and missing-symbol
coverage, with a new predeclared experiment. Do not retune the rejected centroid
head or this statistic on scored B8/development data. Auxiliary rasters require
native-label review, duplicate-safe source planning and unresolved licensing
limits before use. Fresh independently reviewed natural chords in both chart
styles and app runtime/Pencil verification remain promotion requirements.
