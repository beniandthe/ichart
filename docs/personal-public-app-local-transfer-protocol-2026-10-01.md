## Question and limitations

Does the fixed local anchored residual head improve on shared and linear
anchored readings when support enters through the actual app's profile storage
normalizer, for two product-derived lesson sets across eight public writers?

This run tests isolated public characters, not natural musical chords, grouping,
Pencil responsiveness or user-agnostic shipping quality. These eight writers
have already been observed in development. Their results are not a fresh or
sealed evaluation. The 17 retained natural targets lack independently frozen
glyph identity/ownership and remain connection evidence only.

The retained model metadata binds the operational package and anchors, but the
original fit receipt binding source and training-writer membership is missing.
The 32/8/20 split is the historical declared split reproduced by the strict
adapter, not new proof of model training membership. No reserved-writer raster,
encoder call, prediction or personal fit occurs in this experiment.
