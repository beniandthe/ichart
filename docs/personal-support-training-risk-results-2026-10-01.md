## Meaning and next work

The fixed taught-only mixture cannot repair untaught-vs-untaught ordering:
p(u)-p(v)=(1-g)(q(u)-q(v)). Together with the training harm, this supports
changing the hypothesis rather than adjusting personalization strength.
The next separately specified mathematical core transports all class centroids
using lesson evidence and adds a baseline-margin preservation objective plus
an unrelated-writer support control. It is not fitted on actual data yet.

Fresh natural chords in both chart styles, genuine new writers, application
runtime parity and Pencil interaction remain required. The app recognizer,
grouper, profiles and ink were not changed by this diagnostic. No device build,
install, export, commit, push or release is established by this result.
