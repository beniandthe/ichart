Handwrite clear, reusable music charts on iPad, then edit, transpose, and export them for your next rehearsal or gig.

Hi, I’m Beni—a gigging musician and software engineer. I built iChart because paper charts are fast to write, but making changes, transposing them, and keeping everything organized takes time. I wanted to keep the speed and freedom of handwriting while making those charts editable and reusable.

My goal is simple: help musicians spend less time rewriting charts and more time making music. Whether you’re preparing for a gig, leading a rehearsal, or teaching a lesson, I want iChart to be a practical tool you can keep coming back to.

Write, reuse, and share:
- Create Simple Chord Sheet and Rhythm Section Sheet charts.
- Handwrite chord symbols with Apple Pencil, review them, and place them on your chart.
- Add repeat ranges by selecting measures with the Repeats tool.
- Add form markings, text notes, meter, and handwritten rhythm cues.
- Duplicate charts and transpose chord symbols for different keys or instruments.
- Export readable PDFs while keeping your original chart editable.

Start free and keep using it:
iChart’s free Basic plan has no time limit and requires no purchase or subscription. It includes the core chart-writing tools, up to three saved local charts, and unlimited PDF exports. A free account is required for secure sign-in, password recovery, and support, and to connect any optional Pro access and cloud backups to the right person.

Optional iChart Pro:
Pro adds unlimited local charts, Projects, cloud backup and restore, and Forums for sharing reviewed community chart PDFs.
