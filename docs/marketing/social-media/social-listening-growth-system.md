# iChart Social Listening And Community Growth System

Status: launch-week operating loop
Started: 2026-08-05
Primary accounts: `@useichart` on Instagram, TikTok, and Threads
Primary landing page: `https://useichart.com`

## Purpose

Grow iChart by showing up where working musicians are already talking about
paper charts, iPad chart apps, transposition, set prep, wedding gigs, teaching,
and bandleader chart distribution.

This is not an ad-reply system. It is a listening and relationship system:
answer helpfully, learn the exact words musicians use, and turn real pain
points into better posts, replies, website copy, App Store copy, and product
follow-up.

## Engagement Rules

- Lead with the musician problem, not the product.
- If posting as the founder, disclose it warmly: "Hi! I built an app, iChart..."
- For product-introduction replies, a casual "Shameless plug here:" setup is
  acceptable when the thread naturally invites tools, gigs, charts, or builder
  context. Keep the pitch transparent, human, and tied to the exact problem.
- Do not drop a link unless it directly answers the question or someone asks.
- Do not criticize iReal Pro, forScore, OnSong, Finale, Sibelius, or MuseScore.
- Skip etiquette-only iPad-on-stage or gear threads unless chart creation,
  organization, set prep, transposition, or band chart distribution is explicit.
- Keep replies short. One practical paragraph beats a sales essay.

## Curated Follow Lane

Use follows to build a real musician graph, not to manufacture vanity growth.
The follow should make sense if the account owner checks `@useichart` and asks,
"why did this app follow me?"

Daily target:

- Follow 5 to 10 high-fit accounts from the queue or fresh discovery.
- Prefer accounts where we already left a thoughtful comment, where the account
  is clearly active, or where recent posts show chart/set/gig/teacher context.
- Do not mass follow, automate follow/unfollow cycles, buy followers, or ask
  for follow-backs.
- Let the follow stand on fit; comments and Story replies should answer the
  musician context, not ask for reciprocal attention.

Follow tiers:

- Tier 1: chart apps, iPad charts, iReal Pro/forScore/OnSong, transposition,
  gig chart prep, wedding/corporate set materials.
- Tier 2: active bandleaders, MDs, wedding bands, horn players, bassists,
  guitarists, and keyboard players with visible gig-prep content.
- Tier 3: monitor only when the content is general performance, gear, etiquette,
  inspiration, or teaching without chart/set-material evidence.

## Daily Loop

1. Check metrics and inbound activity before outbound work.
2. Search 3 to 5 active lanes.
3. Save useful language exactly, even when we do not reply.
4. Reply only where natural, allowed, and recent enough.
5. Log follows, replies, skips, and content ideas.
6. Turn the best finding into a Story question, Reel hook, carousel, FAQ, or App
   Store/App Store custom product page angle.
