## Actual fit and evidence boundary

Executed the [predeclared experiment](personal-cross-writer-contrastive-protocol-2026-10-01.md),
not another residual-head parameter search. Both arms used the original raster
encoder and affine augmentation, identical seed-29 initial tensors, 30 final
epochs, 32 full-vocabulary batches per epoch, and 6,208 sources from the same
32 training writers. The sole intended objective difference was CE versus
CE plus 0.1 cross-writer contrastive regularization at temperature 0.07.

Both fits completed. Post-fit checks reloaded both model checkpoints and both
6,208-by-128 training-feature bundles. All 30 epoch plans, augmentation hashes,
anchor counts and update/sample counts matched between arms; both changed
encoder parameters. Source/code/protocol identities survived the fit unchanged.
No development query or reserved feature was constructed during fitting.

The derivative evaluation commitment was frozen before development inference.
It bound the two checkpoints, code/runtime, public fixture, exact original app
stored-support geometry, prepared profiles and opaque query schedule. Every
raw/source and stored-support raster matched its retained Swift pixel identity.
Predictions were serialized and hashed before query truth was opened.

The evaluation had 776 distinct session-two query sources, two lesson tasks,
and two model arms: **3,104 retained task/arm attempts, all valid, zero dropped**.
All 97 classes competed. Both tasks used the same fixed anchored-linear learner
and model-specific public training-only anchors; no local/RBF learner, parser,
alias rescue or intended label reached prediction.

These eight writers have already been used in development. This is a fixed
descriptive/futility experiment, not fresh writer or natural-chord accuracy.
The 20 reserved writers remain un-rasterized/un-inferred. The original strong
Core ML encoder is not this new control's parent; its missing original PyTorch
checkpoint was not guessed or reconstructed.

Against its own generic rankings, candidate personalization improved core10 by
four and catalog21 by six correct readings. It nevertheless introduced one
untaught harm in core10 and two in catalog21, failing the predeclared safety
condition. Positive net personalization does not rescue a worse representation.

The app-available 21-codepoint slice has 168 queries. Candidate personalized
counts are 148 versus control 146 in core10, and 151 versus control 149 in
catalog21. This slice is reported, not used to replace the predeclared gate:
candidate generic counts are lower (144 versus 146), full-vocabulary personalized
counts are lower, and individual-writer/untaught safety also fails.

**Disposition: reject this fixed candidate for advancement; no retuning on
these outcomes and no reserved-writer evaluation to rescue it.** The result
rejects this particular regularizer/configuration, not all metric learning or
optional personalization. It supplies no claim of general recognition improvement.

## Next boundary

The retained decoder fixes real candidate-composition limitations but cannot
repair weak or missing glyph evidence. Further model work needs stronger,
appropriately licensed data coverage and a new frozen hypothesis, not rules
for these observed errors. The benchmark still lacks `# + / ø △`; literal
`b o -` are visual/codepoint proxies. Natural-chord ownership and fresh
multiwriter outcomes in both styles remain unproven.
